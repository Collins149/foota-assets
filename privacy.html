Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Welcome to Football LiveScoreStream ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use our mobile application, which provides football fixtures, live scores, match results, league information, notifications, and related content.

Football LiveScoreStream is provided as a free service. By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for particular data processing, we will seek that consent separately.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account. We do not intentionally ask users to provide their name, phone number, email address, or precise location to use the App.

We do not directly collect personal information through the onboarding questions. Your selected answers about football-following habits and preferences are used within the onboarding interface and are not transmitted to us, provided the App continues to operate as currently designed.

However, third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process certain technical information when you use it. Depending on the services enabled and your device settings, this may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers.
  • Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Basic usage events and interactions with app content.
  • Diagnostic information, if crash reporting or diagnostic services are enabled.
  • Push notification tokens used to deliver notifications.

We do not use the App to collect precise GPS location information. Technical information processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective privacy policies and configurations.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App and its integrated services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying football fixtures, scores, results, and league data.
  • Delivering match-related notifications when enabled.
  • Displaying advertisements and measuring advertising performance.
  • Maintaining the functionality, stability, and security of the App.
  • Understanding aggregate usage, where analytics services are enabled.
  • Detecting technical problems and improving the user experience.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services to provide certain features. These services may process technical information as necessary to operate their features.

Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore may be used to deliver football match and league data. The App may also increment a match-view counter when a match is opened. This counter is intended to measure match views and is not designed by itself to identify individual users. We cannot guarantee that technical records maintained by the underlying service contain no additional technical information.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver push notifications. Notification delivery may involve a registration token or other technical identifiers associated with your app installation. You can disable notifications through your device settings or any notification controls provided within the App.

Google AdMob

Google AdMob may display advertisements within the App. It may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and ad interactions for advertising delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement. Personalised advertising may be used where permitted by applicable law and your consent or device settings.

Third-Party Video Providers

If the App provides links to or playback of football video streams, the relevant stream may be hosted by a third-party provider. When you open or play such content, that provider may receive your IP address, device information, and technical request data. We do not control the privacy practices, availability, or security of external servers.

Other Services

Additional services, such as analytics or crash-reporting tools, may be used if they are integrated into the App. We will update this policy when material changes to our data practices require it. We do not claim to use services such as Facebook Analytics, OneSignal, or Firebase Crashlytics unless they are actually enabled in the App.

For more information about Google's data practices, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
